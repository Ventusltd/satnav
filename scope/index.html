SatNav

(concept only stage)

On-road and authorised off-road navigation for electricity infrastructure.

A proposed mobile browser companion connecting mapped substations, power lines and energy projects with location awareness, route following and field observations. Designed to build upon GlobalGrid2050's existing engineering and geospatial tools.

Explore GridAtlasOpen Pipeline News

Proposed capabilities

01 / Locate

Device-based location, reported GPS accuracy, compass bearing and distance to a selected asset or authorised access point. Location remains on the device by default.

02 / Navigate

Follow a validated route with distance remaining and deviation from the route. Public-road routing and authorised last-mile navigation are separate capabilities.

03 / Observe

Explore surrounding grid infrastructure and terrain, and optionally record or export field observations locally.

04 / Integrate

Use existing project identities, network geometry, terrain information and engineering calculations without creating a competing design platform.

Geographical scope

England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Data coverage, accuracy, access rights and coordinate systems must be verified for each region. Nationwide routing and offline availability are not currently claimed.

Development priorities

  1. Mobile location and compass prototype, with accuracy and permission controls.
  2. Route following and integration with existing mapped asset and design information.
  3. Authorised last-mile routing and offline coverage where appropriate data and permissions are available.

A mapped asset is not an authorised entrance. Routes require appropriate access permissions and field safety assessment. Straight-line bearing and distance are not navigable routes.

SatNav is a concept, not an operational navigation service, survey instrument or approved engineering design. Open-source project · GlobalGrid2050
