Capabilities and development scope

01 / Locate

Device-based location, reported GPS accuracy, true bearing and straight-line distance to a selected point are available in the test preview. Location stays on the device.

02 / Navigate

GridAtlas destination selection and selected-line positioning are in development. Validated route-following and authorised last-mile navigation are later capabilities, not available in this preview.

03 / Observe

Explore surrounding grid infrastructure through the existing connected tools. Local field observations and terrain-assisted workflows are future capabilities.

04 / Integrate

Use existing project identities, network geometry, terrain information and engineering calculations without creating a competing design platform.

Geographical scope

England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. Data coverage, accuracy, access rights and coordinate systems must be verified for each region. Nationwide routing and offline availability are not currently claimed.

Development priorities

  1. Available for testing: mobile location and compass prototype, with accuracy and permission controls.
  2. Milestone 1: select and confirm a GridAtlas or Vicinity destination, then launch the compass.
  3. Milestone 2: live measured position and accuracy alongside a selected transmission line.
  4. Later: validated route following, authorised last-mile routing and offline coverage, subject to evidence and permissions.

A mapped asset is not an authorised entrance. Routes require appropriate access permissions and field safety assessment. Straight-line bearing and distance are not navigable routes.

SatNav is a development preview, not an operational navigation service, survey instrument or approved engineering design. Open-source project · GlobalGrid2050
