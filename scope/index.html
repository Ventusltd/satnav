GLOBALGRID2050

SatNav

(concept only stage)

A thin, browser-based SCADA routing and field navigation companion for the United Kingdom. Connect the tools already built; develop only the missing navigation layer. No installation or duplicated engineering platform.

Purpose

Choose an existing asset or a pre-designed route, locate yourself using permissioned phone GPS, follow the route and record field observations. Keep route planning and electrical engineering with their existing owners. A mapped asset is not a verified entrance or permission to enter.

Reuse before building

  • GridAtlas and Vicinity: native infrastructure layers, nearby asset search, identity, voltage tags and SCADA map presentation. Existing transmission and distribution coverage is inherited, not claimed complete; 11 kV UKPN coverage subject to source checks.
  • See Through: mapped substation visualisation, radar-like asset exploration, MapLibre and 3D reference; its 5,800-record snapshot is not a live sensor or an access register.
  • Ventus Grid Engine and Grid Distance Maths: reuse canonical distance, bearing, spatial indexing and engineering context. Corridor estimates are not traversable routes.
  • GIS SLD Sandbox / Solar Design Studio: consume the existing read-only route geometry; return recorded field observations, not a new design engine.
  • LiDAR: reuse existing terrain format, slope methods and data provenance; no assumption of uniform UK coverage.
  • Spider and Kuiper: discover owners, dependencies, duplicated mathematics, evidence and exact source versions. Check current source, licence and tests before selective reuse.

Build only the gap

  1. Mobile GPS, accuracy and stale-fix handling; local-only position by default; compass bearing clearly distinguished from travel heading.
  2. MapLibre and asset/route adapters; selected destination and verified access point kept separate.
  3. Route-following, cross-track error, travelled distance, optional local GPX/GeoJSON/CSV export; smartphone measurements are not survey-grade.
  4. Optional public-road router, only with coverage, service terms and explicit location-sharing consent. No straight-line fallback described as a route.
  5. Optional last-mile A* for authorised field travel where terrain, barriers, access and coverage are known. Synthetic routes are non-navigable. Offline packs only where rights and completeness permit.

England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland are in scope; use nation-specific coverage, CRS and data rights. Public or owner-authorised access is necessary but not sufficient to establish safe passage. No inference of entry into substations or live electrical sites. Cable design, nationwide off-road cost-surface generation and full offline coverage are later, conditional extensions.

Development and acceptance

Three-agent swarm: Agent 22 owns contracts, UI and integration; Agent 23 owns existing terrain/asset adapters, rights and route cross-review; Agent 24 owns routing, cross-track and route-following. Use isolated branches, reciprocal review and reproducible CI, with exact commit receipts and independent acceptance checks. Preserve GridAtlas, Pipeline News and existing immutable releases. No live GPS upload by default.

Planning targets from 24 November 2026: concept page first; first phone compass/route-following candidate within 1–2 development days, integrated pilot within 3–5 development days, conditional on CI, source rights and handset tests. Full UK offline and last-mile coverage remain unestimated pending coverage audit. Targets are not promises. This page is not a released navigation product.
